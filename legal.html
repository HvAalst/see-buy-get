Legal questions — for the lawyer

Every question about the law that came up while building SeeBuyGet, in one place. Each says when it comes up in the product, for which countries, a case to make it concrete, what the product does until it is answered, and where it was raised. Nothing here is decided by the product: where a question is open, the product takes the cautious reading and says so on the screen.

Kept with the code (legal.html). Last brought together 29 October 2026, the air questions included.
