SBG API for businesses and fleets

Your own system — an ERP, a warehouse system, a transport management system — posts loads, sends orders in, reads your plan and takes loads off the board, and is told every step back. Through the same checks as the Business app and the Fleet app: a key can do nothing the app would refuse.

Quickstart

Make a key in the app, put it in the x-sbg-key header, call.

  • A business: Business app → Business details → Keys for your own systems. An owner makes it.
  • A fleet: Fleet app → Dashboard → Your own system → Keys for your own system. Any manager makes it.
curl -s http://localhost:3000/v1/sbg/api/business/loads \
  -H 'x-sbg-key: bk_test_…'

Start with a test key: it runs every check a live one does and keeps nothing, so you can send the same load or the same orders as often as it takes.

Keys and modes

One key per system that holds it. Shown once, kept by us only as a hash, taken away at any time.

Whose it is

A key starts with whose it is and which world it points at: bk_live_… and bk_test_… are a business's, fk_live_… and fk_test_… a fleet's. The company is in the key's record on our side, never in what you send: a key cannot act for anybody else.

Live and test

A live key acts. A test key runs every check — the fields, the trade term, the contract, the driver's vehicle — and keeps nothing: no load is posted, no order placed, nothing taken. Every answer says which: "test": true or false.

Who makes them

A business's owners make and take away its keys; a fleet's keys, any manager. Each act is recorded under the person's name, and the app lists every key: who made it, when it was last used, who took it away. What a key does is recorded under the key's own name — its label and last four characters — so the people in your company can tell your system's acts from their own.

Lost a key? Make a new one and take the old one away. There is nothing to recover: we never kept it.

For a business

Read your loads, post a load, withdraw one nobody has taken yet.

Your loads

GET/sbg/api/business/loads  GET/sbg/api/business/loads/{freightId}

Exactly as your Loads screen reads them: status, carrier, delivery, a problem reported. A load another business posted is not found.

Withdraw a load

POST/sbg/api/business/loads/{freightId}/withdraw  { "reason": "The buyer cancelled" }

Only while nobody has taken it. Once a carrier has it, a person withdraws it in the Business app, where the carrier is told.

For a fleet

Read your orders and your day's plan, send orders in, read the board for a driver, take a load at its price.

Your orders and your plan

GET/sbg/api/fleet/orders?from=&to=  GET/sbg/api/fleet/plan?day=YYYY-MM-DD

Exactly as your Orders screen and your plan board read them. Accepting a plan is a person's, in the app.

Send orders in

POST/sbg/api/fleet/orders

Up to 500 orders under one of your contracts, each read exactly as a line of an uploaded order sheet: the same columns, the same checks, every refusal said in words, order by order. One order's problem does not stop the others.

{
  "contractId": "ctr_…",
  "pickupLabel": "Industrial Area yard", "pickupLat": -1.31, "pickupLng": 36.85,
  "orders": [
    { "reference": "WS-10432", "goods": "Bricks, 4 pallets", "weightKg": 4000,
      "deliverTo": "Plot 12, Kitengela", "lat": -1.478, "lng": 36.962,
      "deliverOn": "2026-10-21", "window": "8-12", "contact": "J. Mwangi",
      "phone": "+254700000000", "packaging": "palletised", "instructions": "Crane offload" }
  ]
}

The answer: imported, how many were ok, duplicates and problems, and a row per order with its verdict and its problems in words. A test key answers the same and places nothing (imported: false).

The board, for one of your drivers

GET/sbg/api/fleet/board?driverDid=

Your drivers on a shift, and for the one you name, every load on the open board: what that driver would earn on it, whether they can take it, and every reason why not. Where your drivers are is in the answer only when your fleet sends that to its own system (see Messages).

Take a load at its price

POST/sbg/api/fleet/loads/{freightId}/take  { "driverDid": "did:glos:…" }

For one of your drivers on a shift, with every check the app makes when your office puts a load on a driver. The answer is the load as your Earnings screen reads it, with its earning. Not an order under a contract (those are put on a truck in the Fleet app) and not a load posted for a price to be agreed. A test key answers whether the driver could take it, why not, and what it would pay — and takes nothing.

What a key never does

  • Agree a price. A load posted to negotiate is agreed by a person.
  • Accept a plan. Your plan for the day is accepted in the Fleet app.
  • Anything about money. No payout, no balance, no bank detail.
  • Anything about people. Who drives for you, who is on the roster, who joins your business.

Messages to your system

Give your system's address in the app and it is told each step, signed with your own secret.

  • A business (Business details → Your system): a carrier took a load, gave it back, picked it up, could not hand it over, delivered it; it was cancelled; a problem reported, and decided. Each carries the load as your Loads screen shows it. Nothing about money.
  • A fleet (Dashboard → Your own system): an order came in, changed, left your Orders, was cancelled; a load put on a driver, taken, given back, picked up, not handed over, delivered, withdrawn, a problem reported and decided; a vehicle broke down, how it ended, its loads moved; space booked on a run, given back, the run called off; the day's plan accepted. A load carries its earning only where SeeBuyGet pays you.
  • Both: on the road — load.at_border, load.border_cleared, load.at_hub — when the driver carrying a load says it is at a border post, has cleared it, or is at a hub. Each carries the step as passage (kind, post or hub, at, and on clearing the minutes it waited, waitedMin) beside the load; never the driver, never where their phone was. A business's load also carries passages, every step in order, and — while it is on the road across a border — crossing: where it has got to, the wait it is allowed and what that rests on (basis measured over so many crossings at that post in the last 90 days, or standard), and expectedAt. A step said with no signal is dated by the driver's phone and arrives when the signal does.
  • Where a fleet's vehicles are — vehicle.position — only once a manager turns it on, every 1, 5 or 15 minutes a vehicle, only while a driver is on a shift in it. Each driver is told so in their app. A position is tried once; the next takes its place.

Checking it came from us

HMAC-SHA256 over the exact body, with your secret, hex-encoded, in x-sbg-signature. The event is in x-sbg-event; x-sbg-delivery is the same on every try — be idempotent on it.

const expected = crypto.createHmac('sha256', SECRET).update(rawBody).digest('hex');
if (!crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(expected), Buffer.from(received))) return res.sendStatus(401);

Tries, and giving up

Answer 2xx within thirty seconds. Otherwise we try again after five minutes, fifteen, an hour, three, six, twelve and twenty-four: eight tries over about two days, then the message is given up and your company is told in its bell and by email, at most once a day. In the app every message says whether it arrived, and Send again sends the same one — same id, same body — to the address and with the secret you have now.

Retrying safely

Send an Idempotency-Key header with any write — posting a load, withdrawing one, sending orders, taking a load — and a retry with the same name and the same body gets the first answer back, with "replayed": true, instead of doing it twice. A name belongs to the key that used it: two systems choosing the same one cannot collide.

Errors

A refusal is a problem document, with a code to branch on and a sentence to show a person:

{ "type": "https://glos.dev/problems/INVALID_STATE", "title": "Not allowed in the current state",
  "status": 400, "code": "INVALID_STATE",
  "detail": "A carrier has this load (claimed) — withdraw it in the Business app, where the carrier is told" }
CodeWhen
UNAUTHORIZED · 401No key, a key that is not ours, or one taken away.
FORBIDDEN · 403A business's key at a fleet's door, or the other way; an order under a contract a key may not take.
NOT_FOUND · 404Not yours: a load, an order, a driver.
INVALID_INPUT · 400A field, in words, with field naming it.
INVALID_STATE · 400Not now: already withdrawn, a carrier has it.
TERM_NOT_ACTIVE · 400A trade term not switched on for that country and direction.
NEGOTIATION_REQUIRED · 400A load whose price is to be agreed, by a person.
RATE_LIMITED · 429Over this key's limit; retryAfterSec says when.
API_VERSION_RETIRED · 410A version that has stopped; see Versions.

Limits

Each key has its own limit, counted per minute and per call: 120 reads, 60 reads of the board, 30 writes. Two systems with two keys never share one.

Versions and notice

Every answer says which version gave it: x-sbg-api-version: 1. A version that would change what a call means is a new one beside the old, never the old one changed.

  • A year's notice. A version keeps working at least twelve months after it is announced as stopping.
  • Told twice. Every company holding a live key is emailed — a business's owners, a fleet's contact email — and told in its bell when it is announced, and again a month before it stops.
  • Said on every answer. Once a version is set to stop, its answers carry Deprecation and Sunset, so a system that reads no email still finds out.
  • After it stops, a call to it is refused with API_VERSION_RETIRED.
