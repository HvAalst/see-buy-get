## BRAZIL
1. **International regime: ATIT** (ALADI partial-scope agreement; Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay). Brazil promulgated it by Decreto 99.704/1990 (https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1990/decreto-99704-20-novembro-1990-342309-anexo-pe.pdf). Spanish text: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/20787/texto. Its **Cap. III, arts. 36–57**, covers rail freight:
   - Full-wagon loads only (art. 37).
   - The single compulsory form is the "Conocimiento – Carta de Porte Internacional – TIF". The treaty requires only "todos los datos que en la misma se requieran" (all data the form asks for), on a nationally approved form (art. 42).
   - Three originals; the first is "negociable" (negotiable) (art. 43).
   - Successive railways are jointly bound, and the destination railway is liable (art. 51).
   - Residual law is "el Derecho del país en que el titular hace valer sus derechos" (the law of the country where the claimant asserts its rights) (art. 41.3).
   - It sets no liability cap or claim deadline.
   - **In Brazil the form is the TIF/DTA** (waybill combined with the customs transit declaration), under IN SRF 12/1993: 4 copies, copy 1 negotiable, copy 4 the transit declaration (https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=353152).
   - A revised Cap. III was agreed at expert level in July 2026; it is not in force (https://prensamercosur.org/2026/07/12/modernizacion-del-atit-se-alcanzo-consenso-sobre-un-capitulo-clave-para-el-transporte-ferroviario-internacional-de-mercancias/).
   - **No cross-border rail freight runs today.** Uruguaiana–Paso de los Libres has been idle since 2017. The Malha Oeste line to Corumbá saw its last train on 1 Dec 2025 and its concession ended 30 Jun 2026.
2. **National law**
   - **Decreto 1.832/1996** (https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=52591):
     - art. 20: "O Conhecimento de Transporte é o documento que caracteriza o contrato de transporte" (the waybill is the document that evidences the contract).
     - arts. 21–22: the shipper answers for its declarations.
     - art. 32: liability is limited "ao valor declarado pelo expedidor" (to the shipper's declared value).
   - **ANTT Res. 5.944/2021**, the rail freight users' regulation (still in force; replaced Res. 3.694/2011): https://anttlegis.antt.gov.br/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&link=S&tipo=RES&numeroAto=00005944&seqAto=000&valorAno=2021&orgao=DG/ANTT/MI&cod_modulo=161&cod_menu=7796
     - art. 2 VI: defines the conhecimento.
     - art. 23: lists essential **contract** clauses, not waybill fields.
     - art. 12 §3: the declared value "obrigatoriamente constante do Conhecimento" (must appear on the waybill).
     - A new regulation, "RSF 1", was approved at hearing stage on 22 Oct 2026; it has no number yet.
   - **Civil Code** (https://www.projuris.com.br/ccc/cc-comentado-do-transporte-de-coisas-art-743-ao-art-756/):
     - art. 743: goods described by "natureza, valor, peso e quantidade" (nature, value, weight, quantity); the consignee at least by name and address.
     - art. 744: the carrier issues the conhecimento on receipt.
     - art. 750: liability limited to the value on the conhecimento.
   - **Particulars:** not prescribed by rail law. In practice the CT-e schema fixes them.
3. **Electronic note: CT-e model 57** (Ajuste SINIEF 09/2007). It replaced the rail paper models 11 and 27. It is "de existência apenas digital" (digital only), valid through the issuer's digital signature plus the tax authority's authorisation.
   - Mandatory for "modal ferroviário" since **1 Dec 2012** (Ajuste SINIEF 18/2011; https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=261).
   - No paper printout (DACTE) is needed on the train when an MDF-e (electronic cargo manifest) covers it (https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=245708).
   - The CT-e 4.00 schema has a rail group `ferrov` holding: traffic type `tpTraf` (own line, interline, road–rail), interline detail `trafMut` (who invoices, which railway issues, the railways involved), and the client contract id `fluxo`. Foreign addresses are allowed (code 9999999 / EX).
4. **Liability:** presumed fault, from receipt to delivery. Capped at the declared value; ANTT art. 12 §3 adds lost profits and direct damages. Total loss is presumed after 30 days. Hidden damage must be reported within 10 days (Civil Code art. 754). Claims prescribe in 1 year (Decreto 2.681/1912 art. 9; https://www.normasbrasil.com.br/norma/decreto-2681-1912_28556.html).

## RUSSIA
1. **International regime**
   - **SMGS party:** listed in the OSJD SMGS text as at 1 Jul 2026 (https://osjd.org/api/media/resources/3921?action=download).
   - **OTIF full member:** acceded 2009, in force 1 Feb 2010. It applies CIM only on three ferry-terminal sections: Baltiysk, Ust-Luga and Kavkaz station–Port Kavkaz. It does not apply Appendices A or C–G (https://treatydatabase.overheid.nl/en/Treaty/Details/009240_p; https://otif.org/en/?page_id=51).
   - **EAEU:** Treaty Annex 24, its Annex 2, covers access and a unified tariff, not the contract or the note (https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_476082/581628fba5e8ffc58215106db9f317d3b99cabd0/).
2. **National law: Ustav 18-FZ (2003)**
   - **Art. 25**, as amended by 140-FZ of 7 Jun 2025 (in force 1 Sep 2026; I verified this text myself): "Транспортная железнодорожная накладная формируется в форме электронного документа" (the rail waybill is formed as an electronic document). The waybill and the "квитанция о приеме груза" (receipt for the goods) confirm that the contract was concluded (https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_40444/f80fb1040d67d2454b9440bf657c8046ec972cdd/).
   - **Art. 27 / 98:** false particulars attract a fine of five times the freight charge.
   - **Rules of carriage:** Mintrans **Order 256 of 27.07.2020**, Ch. IV, splits the fields between consignor and carrier (https://legalacts.ru/doc/prikaz-mintransa-rossii-ot-27072020-n-256-ob-utverzhdenii/):
     - Consignor: stations, consignor/consignee, payer, goods, packing, mass, seals, declared value, special statements.
     - Carrier: waybill number, transit-time expiry, charges, date stamps, carrier's remarks.
   - **Forms:** Order 191 of 19.06.2019 sets the original (ГУ-27у), road sheet, stub and receipt (https://legalacts.ru/doc/prikaz-mintransa-rossii-ot-19062019-n-191-ob-utverzhdenii/).
   - **Particulars: prescribed.**
3. **Electronic note**
   - **Since 1 Sep 2026 the default by statute.** The format is set by the tax service (FNS Order КЧ-1-26/297@). It is signed with an enhanced qualified or enhanced non-qualified e-signature of a person. It is filed to the **ГИС ЭПД**, the same state system as the road e-waybill, under exchange rules in Government Decree 470/2026.
   - **Paper only in listed cases** (Mintrans Order 407/2025): emergency, martial-law or counter-terror regimes, military carriage, force majeure.
   - **Before that:** e-waybills via ETRAN, signed electronically (Order 256 paras 113, 117, 153).
4. **Liability**
   - Presumed fault (art. 95). Pays the goods' invoice or declared value with **no weight cap** (art. 96).
   - Delay: 6% of freight per day, capped at 50% (art. 97).
   - A claim to the carrier is compulsory, within 6 months (45 days for fines) (art. 123); suit within 1 year (art. 125; Civil Code art. 797).

## INDIA
1. **International regime:** not in OTIF or OSJD.
   - **Nepal:** the Rail Services Agreement 2004, latest Letter of Exchange in force 9 Jul 2021, opens Indian Railways to "all authorized cargo train operators… public and private" (https://www.indembkathmandu.gov.in/media-detail/214). Its clauses on governing law and documents were not read (the treaty PDF is a scan).
   - **Bangladesh:** railway interchange at Gede–Darsana, Petrapole–Benapole and others, under MoUs; no carriage treaty found.
   - **Pakistan:** the 1976 agreement; trade has been suspended since 2019.
2. **National law: Railways Act 1989** (statute text via courtbook.in mirrors; indiacode was unreachable).
   - **s.64 forwarding note:** made out "in such form as may be specified by the Central Government"; the consignor answers for the correctness of the particulars and indemnifies the railway (https://courtbook.in/railways-act-1989/section-64).
   - **s.65 railway receipt:** issued on loading or acceptance, in the specified form. It is "prima facie evidence of the weight and the number of packages stated therein". For unchecked wagon-loads the burden of proof shifts to the consignor (https://courtbook.in/railways-act-1989/section-65).
   - **Endorsee:** recognised in s.2(12).
   - **RR particulars:** not verified (the Commercial Manual and Goods Tariff were unreachable).
3. **Electronic note: eT-RR ("Electronic Transmission of Railway Receipt")**
   - Prescribed by Rates Master Circular/eT-RR/2019/0, nationwide from 1 Aug 2019 (PIB summary; page returned 403).
   - The RR is generated, transferred and surrendered electronically, by password-protected PDF with OTP (FOIS manuals, seen only in search summaries).
   - FOIS has been linked to the GST e-way bill since Dec 2024 (https://www.mahagst.gov.in/public/uploads/gstnadvisory/1760529969_310.Advisory%20for%20Entry%20of%20RR%20No.eT-RRs%20in%20EWB%20system%20Post%20EWB-FOIS%20Integration.pdf).
   - **Legal basis:** IT Act 2000 s.4. No amendment to s.65 was found.
   - Appears optional (the customer opts in at booking); unconfirmed.
4. **Liability**
   - Liable for loss, damage or non-delivery subject to the s.93 exceptions, and must still prove "reasonable foresight and care".
   - Rs 50/kg cap without a declared value (s.103 + 1990 Rules r.3); the declared value with a percentage charge.
   - Notice within 6 months of entrustment (s.106); Railway Claims Tribunal claim within 3 years (RCT Act s.17).

## CHINA
1. **International regime**
   - SMGS party; not in OTIF.
   - **China–Europe traffic:** CIM/SMGS common note at the Russian, Kazakh and Mongolian borders since 1 May 2017 (CIT presentation: https://uncitral.un.org/sites/uncitral.un.org/files/media-documents/uncitral/en/Webinardematerializationnegotiabletransportdocuments/presentations/erik_evtimov.pdf).
   - **Laos and Vietnam** are OSJD members. The waybill regime for China–Laos and China–Vietnam traffic was not confirmed.
2. **National law**
   - **Railway Law 1990 (amended 2015), art. 11:** 「…货物运单是合同或者合同的组成部分」 (the goods waybill is the contract or part of it) (https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/ztzl/fkyqfgwzxdzt/fztx/flzs/202002/t20200226_1221380.html).
   - **MOT 《铁路货物运输规则》 (Rail Goods Transport Rules), Order 2026 No. 2**, in force 1 May 2026; domestic carriage only (art. 2). I verified the text myself (https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202603/content_7063679.htm). **Art. 12** says the waybill may be on paper "或者电子数据形式" (or as electronic data) and lists the basic particulars:
     1. Shipper and consignee name, address and contact, **"或者凭指示的收货人"** (or a consignee "to order"), plus the person handling it.
     2. Departure and destination stations, sidings, and the carriers they belong to.
     3. Goods name, packaging, number of pieces, weight including packaging.
     4. Date of acceptance, transit period, charges.
     5. Wagon type and number, marked load; container type and number.
     6. Seal and tarpaulin numbers.
     7. Anything else agreed. The parties may agree otherwise.
   - **Related articles:** art. 44 prefers one shared waybill for through traffic between carriers; art. 5 encourages 「创新铁路提单」 (new railway bills of lading).
   - **Repealed:** the 1986-era 《铁路货物运输合同实施细则》 (implementing rules on rail freight contracts), from 1 May 2022 (State Council Order 752).
   - **China State Railway Group's own regulations** (in force 1 Oct 2026) reportedly extend the shipper's claim period "由180天改为三年" (from 180 days to three years). Source is a news reprint only (https://news.sina.cn/gn/2026-10-01/detail-initstui5326790.d.html).
   - **Civil Code:** arts. 832–833 (liability and amount; statutory limits apply) and art. 842 (multimodal: the law of the leg where the loss occurred).
3. **Electronic note**
   - Allowed by art. 12; art. 13 requires online acceptance and e-documents "where able".
   - **First international e-waybill:** 17 Sep 2026, Suifenhe–Grodekovo, after RZD and China Railway agreed 「电子签名互验」 (mutual verification of electronic signatures) (https://www.news.cn/20260917/ca763091108c4424a25acd035d4a2f7b/c.html).
   - **Legal basis:** Electronic Signature Law arts. 3, 4 and 14.
4. **Liability**
   - From acceptance to delivery. Capped at the declared value, or else the limit set by the railway authority; neither cap applies for intent or gross negligence (Railway Law art. 17; Supreme People's Court interpretation).
   - Goods 30 days late may be treated as lost (art. 16).
   - Court limitation 3 years (Civil Code art. 188).
   - **The current amount of the statutory limit was not verified.**

## SOUTH AFRICA
1. **International regime:** not in OTIF or OSJD.
   - **SADC Protocol on Transport, Communications and Meteorology (1996), Chapter 7:** cooperation only, e.g. art. 7.4(1)(c)(i) "development of uniform conditions of railway carriage". It sets no common note or liability regime (https://commons.laws.africa/akn/aa-sadc/act/protocol/1996/transport-communications-and-meteorology/media/publication/aa-sadc-act-protocol-1996-transport-communications-and-meteorology-publication-document.pdf).
   - **Neighbours:** railway-to-railway arrangements with CFM (Mozambique) and Eswatini (2022) and Botswana (in principle, 2022). No text with carriage terms was found.
2. **National law: contract, not statute.**
   - Transnet Freight Rail's customer page: "Please consult our Standard Conditions of Carriage… clauses 24, 25 & 26 which relate to exclusion of liability and requirements for lodgement of liability claims" (https://www.transnet.net/TFR-Customers). The **current text was not retrieved**.
   - **Historical version** (Spoornet 1998 conditions; https://web.archive.org/web/20091023214203/http://www.spoornet.co.za:80/SpoornetWebContentSAP/html/customers/conditions.htm):
     - "Transport Documents" include a combined consignment note, a freight transit order and EDI messages.
     - "not… a common carrier".
     - Carriage "at the sole risk of the owner".
     - Claims lapse after 180 days.
   - **Particulars:** not prescribed by law. The Legal Succession to the SATS Act 9/1989 was not reachable.
3. **Electronic note:** ECTA 25/2002 does not exclude transport documents (https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a25-02.pdf). No specific rail e-waybill was verified.
4. **Liability:** set by contract. Historically a full exclusion even for negligence, with a 180-day claim lapse. The current clauses were not seen.

## ETHIOPIA
1. **International regime:** not in OTIF or OSJD, and not party to the Arab railways agreement.
   - **Ethiopia–Djibouti treaty and EDR's conditions:** not verified. EDR is a bi-national company (Ethiopia 75%, Djibouti 25%) per secondary sources.
   - **Proc. 1048/2017 art. 36(4):** the Ministry negotiates rail agreements with neighbours.
   - **Multimodal Transport Proc. 548/2007** (rail counts as a mode): SDR 835 per package or SDR 2.5/kg; 2-year time bar (https://www.lawethiopia.com/images/federal_proclamation/proclamations_by_number/548.ae..pdf).
2. **National law: Proc. 547/2007, "Carriage of Goods by Land"** (CMR-modelled; I verified the text myself; https://www.lawethiopia.com/images/federal_proclamation/proclamations_by_number/547.ae..pdf).
   - **Scope:** art. 2(8) "vehicle… includes… rail wagons".
   - **Note:** art. 4 "The contract of carriage shall be confirmed by the making out of a consignment note".
   - **Copies:** art. 5, three originals, with printed or stamped signatures allowed.
   - **Prescribed particulars, art. 6(1):** date and place; sender, carrier and consignee; place and date of taking over, and place of delivery; description and packing (and the recognised description for dangerous goods); number of packages, marks and numbers; gross weight; charges; customs instructions.
   - **Where applicable, art. 6(2):** no-transhipment, declared value, insurance, time-limit and route, documents handed over.
   - **Other rules:** art. 7 a note "to order"; art. 12 the note is prima facie evidence.
   - **Repeal:** it replaced the Commercial Code 1960 carriage articles 561–603. Proc. 1048/2017 is regulatory only.
3. **Electronic note:** 547/2007 says nothing about e-form. 548/2007 arts. 4(3) and 5(1) allow electronic signature and issue for multimodal documents. Electronic Transaction Proc. 1205/2020 exists, but its content was not verified.
4. **Liability (547/2007):**
   - Presumed liability; derogations void (art. 42).
   - SDR 835 per package or SDR 2.5/kg, whichever is higher; delay up to the freight (art. 27). Limits lost for wilful misconduct.
   - Notice: 7 days (hidden damage), 15 days (delay). Time bar 2 years (art. 35).

## IRAN
1. **International regime: both systems.**
   - **COTIF:** in force 1 Jul 2006 with no reservations. Applies CIM, CUV, CUI, APTU and ATMF (OTIF INF.2; https://treatydatabase.overheid.nl/en/Treaty/Details/009240_p).
   - **SMGS:** a party in the 1 Jul 2026 text.
2. **National law**
   - **Law on Open Access to the Rail Transport Network (2005), art. 8:** operators are liable "با انعقاد قرارداد حمل و صدور بارنامه" (by concluding a carriage contract and issuing a waybill).
     - Note 2: "برای هر واگن یک بارنامه" (one waybill per wagon).
     - Note 4: the form is uniform and set by the RAI board.
     - Note 1: liability limits by an executive regulation (not found).
     - Sources: https://rah-ahan.ir/مواد-قانون-دسترسی-آزاد-به-شبکه-حمل-و-نقل/ ; https://www.qavanin.ir/Law/PrintText/87708 (content seen only via a search summary).
   - **Commercial Code arts. 377–394** apply generally (Wikisource copy, not an official gazette; https://fa.wikisource.org/wiki/قانون_تجارت_ایران/قرارداد_حمل_و_نقل).
   - **Particulars:** set by the RAI board; the form itself was not found.
3. **Electronic note:** not verified (rai.ir sits behind a browser check).
4. **Liability (Commercial Code):** the goods' value unless otherwise agreed (art. 386); delay capped at the total-loss amount (art. 387); one-year limitation (art. 393).

## UAE
1. **International regime:** not in OTIF or OSJD. None of the three agreements below contains carriage, note or liability rules.
   - **Arab railways agreement:** ratified 25 Feb 2011.
   - **GCC General Agreement on the GCC railway:** the UAE's ratification was not verified.
   - **UAE–Oman Hafeet Rail agreement** (signed 21 Jun 2026; Oman Royal Decree 75/2026). Art. 10(4): the developer complies with both states' legislation on freight services (https://qanoon.om/p/2026/rd2026075/).
2. **National law**
   - **Federal Law 8/2020 Regulating Railways** (plus Cabinet Resolution 5/2022) is regulatory. It is "silent" on cargo liability (Al Tamimi: https://turtl.tamimi.com/story/law-update-issue-360-mother-africa-and-transport-and-logistics/page/33).
   - **Commercial Transactions Law, Federal Decree-Law 50/2022** (unofficial English text; https://legaladviceme.com/legislation/172/uae-federal-decree-law-issuing-commercial-transactions-law):
     - art. 271: covers all non-maritime transport.
     - art. 273: the contract can be proved by any means.
     - art. 281(1): particulars **only "if executed"** — parties, places, goods and their value, dates, freight and who pays, and similar.
     - art. 281(2)–(3): the document may be named, to order or to bearer.
   - **Etihad Rail "Standard Haulage Terms" v10 (Feb 2026)** (https://corporate-cdn.etihadrail.ae/uploads/ff1b5e5329d8ded256d71c5ec8a696c0.pdf):
     - **No consignment note:** "adequate forwarding instructions" (cl. 7.1).
     - **Receipt:** a customer-prepared receipt that "shall not evidence the condition, correctness or declared nature, quantity or weight" (cl. 7.2).
3. **Electronic note:** Decree-Law 46/2021 gives electronic documents legal effect (DWF commentary; the official text was unreachable). No rail e-note was found.
4. **Liability**
   - **Statute:** exclusion clauses for loss or damage are void (art. 307). Written limitation clauses are allowed (arts. 308–310). Time bar 6 months domestic, 1 year international (art. 319).
   - **Etihad Rail's terms:**
     - Caps: AED 30,000 per container; 8.33 SDR/kg for cargo damaged with its container; otherwise 25% of annual charges.
     - Damage notice within 2 days; claims within 2 months.

## INDONESIA
1. **International regime:** none. Not in OTIF or OSJD.
   - UU 23/2007 art. 128 and PP 72/2009 art. 7 foresee international services only "berdasarkan perjanjian antarnegara" (on the basis of an agreement between states). None was found.
   - There is no rail border; that is general knowledge, not checked against a source.
   - ASEAN multimodal agreement ratification: not verified.
2. **National law**
   - **UU 23/2007** (https://jdih.sumselprov.go.id/storage/userfiles/UU%20No.23%20TH%202007.pdf):
     - art. 141(3): "Surat angkutan barang merupakan tanda bukti terjadinya perjanjian pengangkutan barang" (the goods waybill is proof of the carriage contract).
     - art. 143: the user answers for the truth of its entries.
   - **PP 72/2009 art. 144(2):** minimum contract contents (https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/36697/PP_No_72_2009.pdf).
   - **PM 48/2014 art. 7(5) — particulars prescribed** (https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/94450/pm_48_tahun_2014.pdf):
     - (a) operator name and address;
     - (b) user name and address;
     - (c) "jenis, karakteristik, dan berat barang" (type, characteristics and weight of the goods);
     - (d) departure and destination stations;
     - (e) dates and times of departure and arrival;
     - (f) agreed tariff;
     - (g) operator's signature.
     - Four copies; one waybill per departure.
   - **KAI's freight terms:** not published.
3. **Electronic note**
   - No rail e-waybill. PM 48/2014 assumes paper copies and signatures.
   - **UU ITE 11/2008:** art. 5(1) makes electronic documents valid evidence; art. 5(4) excepts documents a law requires in written form (https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Republik_Indonesia_Nomor_11_Tahun_2008, original 2008 text).
   - Whether the waybill falls under that exception is a question for counsel.
4. **Liability:** actual loss, excluding lost profit and freight already paid; no monetary cap found (UU 23/2007 art. 158). PP 72/2009 arts. 175–177 set short 7-day windows for claims.

## Across the states (for designing the note)
- **Particulars prescribed by law:** Russia, China, Indonesia, Ethiopia and Iran (by RAI form).
- **Particulars not prescribed:**
  - Brazil: rail law has no field list, but the CT-e schema fixes them in practice.
  - India: the form is "as specified" by the Central Government (not retrieved).
  - South Africa: contract.
  - UAE: only "if executed".
  - ATIT TIF: only the nationally approved form.
- **Electronic note:**
  - Mandatory: Brazil (CT-e) and Russia (from 1 Sep 2026).
  - Allowed: China; India (eT-RR).
  - General e-transaction law only: South Africa, UAE, Indonesia (with a written-form exception), Ethiopia (multimodal only).
- **Negotiable or "to order":** ATIT TIF copy 1 is negotiable; China's art. 12 allows a "to order" consignee; Ethiopia art. 7; UAE art. 281; India recognises endorsees.
- **Where the chosen law sits:**
  - ATIT art. 41.3 points to the forum's law.
  - China's Civil Code art. 842 and Ethiopia's 548/2007 apply the law of the leg where the loss occurred.
