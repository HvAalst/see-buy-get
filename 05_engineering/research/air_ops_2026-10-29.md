### 2. Export acceptance (lodging)

**Published cut-offs.** All are minutes before scheduled departure (STD) unless stated. "BUP" is a built-up pallet or container; "loose" is cargo handed in unbuilt.

**Emirates, Kenya (NBO), effective 1 Oct 2026** **[re-checked]** (P, https://www.skycargo.com/media/orip5thd/01oct2026_lsc_africas_kenya-ke.pdf)
- Passenger flights:
  - General cargo loose: "STD – 4 hours (240 mins)"
  - Priority loose: 2 h, or 4 h if over 100 kg
  - Special Products loose: 2 h
  - BUP: 3 h
- Freighters:
  - General cargo loose: "STD – 5hours (300 mins)"
  - Priority loose: 2 h (4 h if over 100 kg)
  - Special Products: 4 h
- "RCS and Documentation formalities must be completed prior to the above-mentioned latest acceptance timings" (RCS = received, ready for carriage).

**Design consequence:** a cut-off is a row keyed by carrier × station × product × BUP/loose × passenger/freighter × weight threshold. It holds minutes before STD, a separate documents/RCS deadline, the source and the date it takes effect. When the shipper's booking confirmation states an LAT, that LAT wins over the published row.

**Ready for carriage**
- Emirates H: the shipper tenders the shipment "as 'Ready for Carriage' according to… Article 3".
- Article 3 of its conditions: carriage is "subject to the availability of suitable equipment and space"; goods "packed in a manner suitable for carriage by aircraft" and "accompanied by the requisite shipping documents"; each package "legibly and durably marked" with shipper and consignee (P, https://www.skycargo.com/conditions-of-carriage/).
- The clearest definition is Cargolux CoC Art. 1.17. Its service starts on "a valid and legal receipt of a READY FOR CARRIAGE SHIPMENT" (P, https://www.cargolux.com/conditions-of-carriage/).
- Ethiopian's documents per kind (P, FAQ):
  - Perishables: certificate of origin, "Phyto certificate (if the shipment is flower)", packing list, invoice, health certificate.
  - Live animals: "Full quarantine for the AVI", health certificate "as per IATA", the consignee's import permit, CITES where it applies.
  - Pharma: the IATA checklist for temperature-sensitive goods; "Only one specified shipping temperature range is permitted per AWB"; a 24-hour phone contact on the air waybill.
- IATA's Live Animals Regulations and Perishable Cargo Regulations both have a new edition in effect from 1 Jan 2026 (P, iata.org programme pages).
- Kenya (KEPHIS charter Rev 08, uploaded 2026-08) **[re-checked]**: "Phytosanitary inspections for fresh produce — Physical presentation of the consignment, Commercial invoice, Packing list … Flowers … 3 Hours". The phytosanitary certificate is issued within "3 Hours and SLA". The 3-hour inspection belongs in the export timeline before the LAT.

**What happens to a late truck**
- **Emirates Kenya D, no-show** **[re-checked]**: "100% of the total charges" "For all shipments not delivered before the Latest Acceptance Time (LAT) of the scheduled first leg (flight or truck)". The same applies to confirmed bookings "not cancelled or rebooked at least 12 hours before the LAT".
  - Cancellation within 48 h of the LAT: 100% of charges.
  - Low-show (more than 50% lighter, under 24 h notice): 100% on the difference.
  - All of this applies to bookings of 1 t or more, excluding courier and mail.
- **Emirates G:** handing cargo to a truck at an off-airport drop point "does not imply that the cargo is ready to uplift". If acceptance fails, the shipper must make it ready "or return it back at their own cost with no liability to Carrier" **[re-checked]**.
- **Conditions of carriage** (Emirates/Ethiopian 6.3): times are "approximate and not guaranteed". The carrier may "proceed with any flight without all or any part of the cargo". For perishables (Emirates 8.5.1), if a shipment "is delayed… or… threatened with deterioration", the carrier may take steps "including… destruction or abandonment".
- **Missing security status:** if the regulated-agent or known-consignor identifier is missing or not active, "it shall be deemed that no security controls have previously been applied", and the cargo is screened again at the terminal, which costs time (EU 2015/1998 §6.8.3.5, as of 2020-07-02 via legislation.gov.uk).

**Security screening**
- **Kenya: Civil Aviation (Security) Regulations 2025**, Legal Notice 31, in force 3 Mar 2026 (P, https://www.kcaa.or.ke/sites/default/files/regulation/Legal%20Notice%20No.%2031%20-%20Civil%20Aviation%20(Security)%20Regulations%2C%202025.pdf).
  - r.47(1): before accepting cargo, the regulated agent must "establish the credentials of the person who delivers the cargo" and screen cargo "from unknown consignors". It issues a "security status… which shall accompany the cargo".
  - r.49(4): consignments are "delivered by an established employee of a handling agent, aircraft operator or regulated agent", "covered by a valid consignment security declaration", and "checked … no evidence of having been tampered with". The list is joined by "or".
  - Kenya requires a paper consignment security declaration (CSD); Ethiopia too. Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda accept an electronic one (P, IATA e-CSD survey, May 2022 — may be dated).
  - KCAA publishes no known-consignor list.
- **EU:** carriers flying cargo into the EU must be designated ACC3. Cargo already secured by an EU-validated regulated agent (RA3) or known consignor (KC3) need not be screened again (§6.8.3.1).
  - Security status codes: SPX (passenger and cargo aircraft), SCO (cargo-only aircraft), SHR (high risk).
  - Screening method codes: PHS, XRY, EDS, EDD, ETD, VCK, CMD, AOM (§6.3.2.6).
  - Inside the EU, the haulier's vehicle must be "locked or sealed", with a signed haulier declaration (§6.6.1.1). Whether RA3/KC3 validation imposes the same on trucks in Kenya: not verified.
- **US:** 100% screening of cargo on passenger aircraft (P, tsa.gov). Advance data under ACAS is due "no later than prior to loading" (P, 19 CFR 122.48b).

### 3. Import release: from "landed" to "a truck may collect"

**EAC law, binding in KE, UG, TZ and RW; not Ethiopia** (P, EAC Customs Management Act (EACCMA), https://www.mof.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1670501645-EAC_Customs_Management_Act_(Revised)%5B1%5D.pdf; amendment at eala.org)
- s.34: goods must be entered "within twenty one days after the commencement of discharge".
- s.42: goods "not lawfully removed within thirty days" are gazetted. After a further 30 days they are "deemed to have been abandoned … for sale by public auction". Goods of a "perishable nature, or are animals, may be sold … without notice … at any time after deposit".
- s.26: a transit shed is deemed still to be the aircraft, and the shed owner is "responsible and accountable".
- EAC Regulations 2010 (P, https://infotradekenya.go.ke/media/EAC%20Customs%20Management%20Regulations_1.pdf):
  - reg 86: rents "shall be paid before delivery".
  - regs 4–6: officers attend outside hours only on Form C.1, applied for "at least twenty-four hours before". Sundays and public holidays carry a minimum charge of three hours.

**Kenya, JKIA** (P, InfoTradeKenya, run by KenTrade: vaccines procedure 5547 and dogs/cats 1744, modified 29 Nov 2025; fruits 1631, 30 Nov 2025)

Before arrival:
- The Import Declaration Form (IDF) and Unique Consignment Reference (UCR, USD 10) are filed in iCMS, KRA's customs system. For regulated goods the permit must come first, on KenTrade's Trade Facilitation Platform (TFP), "which triggers approval of the IDF".
- Permits by goods:
  - KEPHIS plant import permit: KSh 600, needed "prior to shipment"
  - PPB medicines permit: 0.75% of value
  - DVS animal permit (e.g. VS01): KSh 500
  - Port Health certificate: KSh 1,000

At JKIA, in order:
1. The shed's Customer Care contacts the agent, giving the "Notification of arrival (Release order)". This one form is then stamped by each agency in turn.
2. Documents (notification of arrival and air waybill) are collected at the imports counter.
3. The licensed agent registers the iCMS entry.
4. Duties and levies are paid: Railway Development Levy 1.5%, IDF fee 2.5%, an "airline concession fee" of KSh 250.
5. The entry becomes "under control".
6. The other agencies act:
   - PPB: document check, then physical check. On failure it will "seize the consignment, issue an objection certificate". On success, a "No objection certificate".
   - KEPHIS: inspection booked in its import system. Non-compliant goods are "treated, destroyed or shipped back … at the expense of the importer".
   - DVS: at the Animal Holding Station, ending in "Passed clearance entry stamped release by DVS".
   - KEBS (standards): only without a certificate of conformity.
   - Port Health: approval.
7. KRA verification: the release is issued in iCMS and the status moves "under control → pending removal → removed; Signed release order".
8. The shed's invoice is paid at its cash counter.
9. The shed sends a delivery notification.
10. The Customs Enforcement Unit stamps; the entry is "settled".
11. The shed checks goods against the documents.
12. The customs border-control officer gives "Physical release of consignment".

The iCMS status sequence is a machine-readable gate signal for us.

**Ethiopia, Bole**
- Conditions of carriage Art. 8.1: the carrier "is not liable for non-receipt or delay in receipt" of the arrival notice. Delivery is deemed made when goods are handed to customs (P).
- Customs runs through the electronic single window, https://esw.et/. The name of the customs system itself is not verified.
- EFDA (the food and drug authority), guideline EFDA/GDL/077, effective 29 Aug 2025: Bole is the "Primary entry point"; inspectors review "temperature logger data"; "Pharmaceutical consignments shall be granted the highest clearance priority" (P).
- Collection hours: "Monday to Saturday: 06:00 - 21:00 … Sunday and during Holidays: 06:00 - 17:00" **[re-checked]**. A company collecting needs a "Letter of attorney from Federal Authentication Authority" and a bank delivery order where applicable.
- Typical time: "approximately two weeks", not airport-specific (S, trade.gov, 2024).

**Uganda, Entebbe**
- Manifest reconciliation "is often initiated by airline ground handlers after the aircraft has landed … it may take several hours" (P, URA, 4 Jun 2026 and 14 Aug 2026).
- Permits from MAAIF (plant and veterinary), the National Drug Authority and UNBS go through the Uganda Electronic Single Window (UESW). The declaration is in ASYCUDA World. The release order comes from URA Entebbe (P, ugandatrades.go.ug procedure 254, 12 Jun 2026).
- URA target: "within not more than 2 days" (P, 2023).

**Rwanda, Kigali**
- Steps: MAGERWA arrival notice → single-window declaration (IM4) → payment → RRA inspection (Gold Card and AEO traders get "automatic release") → RICA (plant and animal health) and Rwanda FDA approval → "Exit note - IM" (P, rwandatrade.rw).
- Hours: Days 1–5 07:00–00:00, Day 6 08:00–13:00, Day 7 none.
- Time Release Study, Q1 2024, all offices: 3 d 01 h 02 m. Kigali airport is not reported separately (P).

**The model:** free time, then a rate per kg per day with a minimum per air waybill, plus add-ons for cold rooms, strong rooms, DG and out-of-hours work. Special cargo is excluded from free time or charged from day 0. The free-time clock differs by place:
- hours from an unstated event (JKIA)
- calendar days including holidays (Addis)
- day of acceptance plus working hours (Frankfurt)

So each rule must store its unit, the event that starts the clock, whether it counts calendar or working time, and its exclusions.

### 6. Bookings, the air waybill number, schedules and status

**The air waybill (AWB) number**
- "an AWB number is made of 11-digits", format "000-00000000"; the last digit is a check digit, "unweighted Modulus 7" (P, https://dgautocheck.iata.org/help/AirWaybillnumbervalidation.html).
- "The seven-digit serial number is simply divided by 7 and the resulting remainder is the check digit" (P, Australian Border Force developer reference). So the check digit is always 0–6.
- Airline prefixes, agreed in two handler and port-community tables (P, WFS Copenhagen list, 5 Jul 2025; Cargonaut airline–handler table):

| Airline | Prefix |
|---|---|
| Kenya Airways (KQ) | 706 |
| Ethiopian (ET) | 071 |
| Qatar (QR) | 157 |
| Emirates (EK) | 176 |
| KLM / Martinair | 074 |
| Air France | 057 |
| Turkish (TK) | 235 |
| Lufthansa (LH) | 020 |
| RwandAir (WB) | 459 |
| Astral Aviation | 485 |
| Saudia | 065 |

- Uganda Airlines (109?) and Air Tanzania (197?) appear only in secondary sources: not verified.
- Validate prefixes against a stored table: one blog gave "006" for Air France, but 006 is Delta.
- Master, house and direct waybills are distinct types (P, IATA ONE Record ontology 3.3.0).

**Booking messages**
- The flow is: booking request (FFR) or web portal → booking answer (FFA) → status update "booked" (FSU/BKD) → the airline's booked list to the handler (FBL) (P, IATA Istanbul e-AWB SOP).
- **Name clash:** Cargo-IMP "FBL" means the Freight Booked List, not the FIATA Bill of Lading that your plan lists next. Keep the two names apart in code.
- The electronic air waybill rests on IATA Resolution 672, the Multilateral e-AWB Agreement, which comes into force between an airline and a forwarder at a station "only upon airline sending an Activation Notice" (P, iata.org).
- When the forwarder's waybill data message (FWB) is due varies by station — again data, not code:
  - Entebbe: "at least 360 minutes before delivery of the goods" (P, IATA EBB SOP)
  - Hamburg: 90 min
  - Doha: 5 min
- In the IATA data model, a booking carries an "earliestAcceptanceTime", a "latestAcceptanceTime" ("as per CargoIQ definition") and a "timeOfAvailability". Booking status values are CONFIRMED / REJECTED / QUEUED / DELETED (P, ONE Record 3.3.0).
- From 1 Jan 2026 IATA endorses ONE Record as the preferred standard, and Cargo-XML is "in containment" (no new messages or versions) (P, iata-cargo.github.io release note).
- ONE Record logistics events "MUST NOT be changed after creation", which matches our freeze-and-hash habit (P).

**Status codes, in IATA's own wording** (P, ONE Record code lists, 24 Jun 2025)
- BKD: booked
- FOH: "on hand … pending 'ready for carriage' determination"
- RCS: received and "considered by the carrier as ready for carriage"
- MAN: manifested
- DEP: departed
- ARR: arrived
- RCF: received from the flight
- NFD: "The consignee or the consignee's agent has been notified … of the arrival"
- AWD: "arrival documentation has been physically delivered" to the consignee
- CCD: "cleared by the Customs authorities"
- DLV: delivered
- DIS: discrepancy, including offloaded
- Truck windows also exist as codes: SS and SR (earliest and latest driver reporting time), PA (truck pre-announcement), AG and AH (gate in and gate out).

**Public schedules and tracking, for people to follow by link**
- Ethiopian has a public cargo schedule tool and tracking page. Its conditions of carriage say timetable times are "approximate and not guaranteed" (P).
- Turkish: schedule and tracking pages; robots.txt allows /en/ but disallows /api/.
- Qatar: tracking and schedule exist, but render only with JavaScript.
- KQ: freighter schedule and status pages exist, but robots.txt disallows everything, so link only.
- Cargonaut (Schiphol) sells a licensed tracking API for forwarders and road hauliers: the licensed route, not scraping.
- Booking-confirmation layouts at Emirates, Qatar, AF-KLM, KQ, cargo.one, WebCargo and CargoAi: not verified.

**Truck slot booking at the big hubs — the model to copy**
- **Schiphol** (P, Cargonaut eLink):
  - Each delivery is a "Visit" per handler, with a slot, the regulated agent's code, and AWBs whose waybill data is accepted and marked green "Ready for Take-In".
  - Visits group into a "Trip" with the tractor plate, a second plate from 25 Jun 2025, and a driver card.
  - Gate window: "Visit ingepland tussen 15:00 en 16:00 … aanmelden tussen 14:00 en 20:00" — a visit booked for 15:00–16:00 may check in from 14:00 to 20:00 (20 Aug 2026).
  - The paper slip ends 1 Jun 2026.
  - Imports: "all pickups must be announced in advance"; the pickup turns green only when the shipment is nominated, the handler has sent NFD, and it confirms "available for pickup".
- **Dubai** (P, calogi.com): "only registered vehicles with valid appointments will be allowed entry"; arrive within ±15 min, 30 min grace, amend or cancel up to 2 h before; live since 2019–20.
- **Brussels and Liège:** slot booking on Nallian / BRUcloud since 2018 (S, the vendor).
- No East African handler with truck slot booking was found.

## SYNTHESIS

### A. The "export by air" record should hold
1. **Booking, as copied from the shipper's confirmation:**
   - AWB: a 3-digit prefix checked against a stored airline table, plus an 8-digit serial with the mod-7 check
   - waybill type: master, house or direct; the house number if any
   - airline
   - segments: flight number, date, origin and destination airport, STD in local time with its time zone. Rwanda is UTC+2, the others UTC+3. Ethiopia's local clock is not verified in these sources: ask the user to confirm the international clock.
   - booking status: confirmed / queued (on request) / rejected
   - pieces, weight and volume
   - product family, BUP or loose, passenger or freighter aircraft
   - **the LAT as printed on the confirmation**
   - optional time of availability at destination
2. **A fallback cut-off rule from the data** (carrier × station × product × BUP/loose × aircraft × weight → minutes before STD), with its source, the date it takes effect, and a documents/RCS deadline. Shown as "published by X on date Y — your confirmation's LAT wins".
3. **The cargo terminal as a place:**
   - the airport, and the handler/shed that receives this airline's cargo, as an airline→handler mapping per station with effective dates (it changes: Çelebi Dec 2025, Saudia→Swissport 2026)
   - the drop-off point
   - opening hours
   - DG hours (e.g. IAG: no DG after 15:00)
4. **Security:**
   - the regulated agent who receives the cargo (KE/RA number, EU RA3 number if any)
   - known consignor, yes or no
   - expected security status (SPX/SCO), or "will be screened at the terminal", which adds time
   - whether the CSD is paper or electronic, per country
5. **Documents per kind, each with a status:**
   - export entry in iCMS
   - KEPHIS phytosanitary certificate and inspection booking (3 h standard)
   - DG declaration
   - animal health certificate, import permit, CITES
   - pharma checklist, with one temperature range per AWB
   - the waybill data message sent by the station's lead time
6. **Truck:**
   - plates for tractor and trailer
   - driver and ID
   - KAA permit, if the truck goes past the landside gate
   - seal number
   - reefer set-point and logger, for perishables and pharma

### B. The "import by air" record should hold
1. AWB, flight, scheduled and actual arrival, as entered by the agent or importer.
2. The handling shed (from the customs-shed list).
3. **Release steps as separate, dated, signed events:**
   - notified of arrival (NFD, the "Notification of arrival (Release order)")
   - documents collected
   - customs entry number and status (Kenya: under control → pending removal → removed → settled; elsewhere a release order or exit note)
   - each agency's release the goods kind needs: KEPHIS, DVS, PPB, KEBS, Port Health (Kenya); EFDA (Ethiopia); MAAIF, NDA and UNBS (Uganda); RICA and FDA (Rwanda); TMDA and TBS (Tanzania)
   - handler charges paid
   - delivery order or gate pass reference
   - delivered
4. **The destination of the road leg as a typed place:** consignee door, or "place of quarantine named by the veterinary officer" (animals), or "central vaccine store" (vaccines).
5. **The free-storage rule:** a terminal × cargo kind row holding the free amount and unit, the event that starts the clock (arrival / notified / accepted), calendar or working time, the rate, the minimum, the source and the date. Special cargo is "charged from the start" unless the data says otherwise.
6. **EAC customs clocks** (law, not tariff): 21 days to enter; 30 days to remove, then notice, then deemed abandoned after a further 30; perishables and animals can be sold at any time.

### C. Timers and warnings that cut late trucks and wasted trips
- **Leave-by time** = LAT − drive time − buffer − time still needed at the gate (KEPHIS 3 h if inspection is at the exit point; terminal screening if the cargo is not secured; queueing at peaks). Warn when the truck has not left, when the shipment's documents or RCS items are open close to the LAT, and when the booking is not CONFIRMED (no truck sent on "queued").
- **Rebook/cancel line:** for carriers that publish one (Emirates, 1 t and more), warn before "12 hours before LAT", after which a no-show costs 100% of the charges. Store this as carrier data.
- **Peak season flag**, Valentine's, Mother's Day and Women's Day: prompt to book capacity early; warn that offload risk is higher. Never predict it.
- **Free time countdown** at export (KQ 2013: 7 days dry, 8 h cold) and at import (48 h, calendar days, special cargo charged from day 0). Each countdown shows its source and date.
- **Weekend and holiday arrival flag** for imports needing PPB, DVS or KEBS (Mon–Fri only at JKIA). Mention overtime attendance (Form C.1, 24 h ahead) and the WHO rule of no weekend vaccine arrivals.
- **Permit before departure:** for regulated goods, warn if no permit reference exists before the flight's STD at origin (Kenya: the IDF waits on the permit; KEPHIS needs it "prior to shipment").
- **System outage notices** (KRA iCMS, RRA systems): data rows with a time window, shown on affected records.
- **A slot or window per terminal** — pre-announcement plus a window rule as data (Schiphol −1 h/+4 h; Dubai ±15 min plus 30 min grace) — for when a terminal publishes one. None in East Africa yet.

### D. The gate on the import road leg
- The road leg cannot be **dispatched** until "released" is recorded: the customs release (release order or exit note) plus every agency release the goods kind needs, plus the handler's delivery order or gate pass. Each is entered with a reference and who entered it.
- Or the **importer's override**, as for rail: signed, with a reason, and an acknowledgement that the truck's waiting time is the importer's.
- For live animals, the override can never change the destination from the vet-named place of quarantine.
- Recording "notified of arrival" alone is never enough; that is where rail differs. Ethiopian's conditions of carriage even disclaim the notice itself.

### E. What we must never claim
- That a flight will leave, leave on time, or carry the cargo. Timetables are "approximate and not guaranteed", and carriers may fly "without all or any part of the cargo".
- That a truck reaching the drop-off means the cargo is accepted. Emirates G: drop-off "does not imply that the cargo is ready to uplift".
- That a release will come, or by when. Service-charter times are targets, shown as such.
- That free time or charges are a bill. They are the handler's; we show a dated published figure.
- That cargo is "secure". Only a regulated agent or known consignor issues a security status.
- That a temperature was held without a logger reading.
- That we, or our driver, are a regulated agent, a handler, or the carrier by air.
