Track your parcel

Enter your tracking key to see where your GLOS PUDO parcel is and which point to collect it from.

How to track a GLOS PUDO parcel

Your tracking key looks like T-XXXXXXXX and is sent to you by the store or sender. Enter it above to see the live status. Tracking is public — you don't need an account or the app. When your parcel is ready for pick-up, you'll also see the shelf slot and the pick-up point to collect from; bring your separate collection code to the shop.

What the statuses mean

Created
The label has been generated; the parcel is about to enter the network.
At PUDO
Accepted at a pick-up / drop-off point, waiting for transport.
On the way
On a vehicle, moving between points or through a sort hub (MDP).
Ready for pick-up
Shelved at your destination point — collect any time the shop is open, with your collection code.
Delivered
Collected and confirmed. The journey is complete.
