What is GLOS PUDO?

GLOS PUDO is a pick-up / drop-off (PUDO) parcel network built for emerging markets. Instead of delivering to every door, parcels are sent to a nearby pick-up point — a local shop — where the buyer collects them with a code. Independent drivers move parcels between points, and regional sort hubs (MDPs) connect different areas. It reuses the GLOS logistics backbone, so every parcel is tracked end to end.

How it works

  • Track a parcel: enter your tracking key (for example T-XXXXXXXX) under “Follow your parcel” on this page.
  • Collect a parcel: go to your chosen pick-up point and show your collection code — collect any time the shop is open.
  • Send a parcel: drop it at a PUDO point; it travels point → hub → point to the destination pick-up point.
  • Open-data pricing: every PUDO, hub and driver publishes its own rate, so you always see a competitive price.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track my GLOS parcel?

Enter your tracking key on the home page under “Follow your parcel”. You'll see the current status (created, in transit, ready for pick-up, delivered) and which point to collect from. No account or app is needed.

How do I collect a parcel at a PUDO point?

Go to the destination pick-up point shown in your tracking and show your collection code. The shopkeeper finds your parcel on the shelf and hands it over.

How much does PUDO delivery cost?

Pricing is open data: each pick-up point, sort hub and driver sets its own rate, and a shipment is quoted from the points it passes plus the distance. You can see the going rate for your area, so you always settle at a competitive price.

How do I become a PUDO pick-up point?

Tap “Join the network”, choose “Become a PUDO point”, verify your phone, set your shop's location on the map, and you can start holding parcels for collection — earning a fee per parcel.

Can I sell online and use GLOS PUDO for delivery?

Yes. Vendors wire their checkout into GLOS click-and-collect: the buyer picks a pick-up point, and you generate a label plus a tracking key and collection code for the order.
